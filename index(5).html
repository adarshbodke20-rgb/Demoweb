01 / The book

A question worth examining.

Why did the idea of God become such a powerful part of human life?

Anatomy of God approaches this question through curiosity about humanity: how people make sense of uncertainty, how beliefs take shape, and how ideas about the divine become connected with everyday life.

This is a personal, independent writing project. It does not ask readers to accept a conclusion without thought. It invites them to examine the questions, consider the explanations and think for themselves.

Human belief

How people form beliefs and give meaning to experiences they do not fully understand.

Psychology

How human needs, patterns of thought and the search for protection may shape belief.

Morality

How ideas about right and wrong become connected with religion, society and human life.

Human origins

How early people may have interpreted the natural world and the unknown around them.

A thought to begin with

Why do we believe?
Why do we follow?

And why do we sometimes accept ideas without examining them? Asking questions does not have to mean rejecting something. It can be the beginning of understanding.

02 / Beyond the title

Keep asking why.

This book opens a conversation rather than closing one. Readers may agree, disagree, question its reasoning or bring different perspectives.

As the project develops, this space can feature supplementary essays, references, clarifications and responses to thoughtful questions.

03 / The author
AB

Adarsh Bodke

Adarsh Bodke is a biology student from India. His interests extend beyond his academic studies into science, human nature, philosophy and the many questions surrounding life and human existence.

He has a habit of asking one question about almost everything: Why?

Why do we believe? Why do we follow? Why do we accept? Why do we consider certain things right or wrong? And, perhaps most importantly, why don't we question them more often?

For him, asking why is not about disagreeing with everything, but about understanding things beyond their familiar explanations.

Anatomy of God is his first book, an independent writing project developed alongside his academic pursuits. It reflects his personal curiosity about humanity, belief and the origins of the idea of God.
